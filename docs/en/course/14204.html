Must-see spots in Kamakura

  • Tsurugaoka Hachimangu Shrine (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): Walk the raised Dankazura path to the main shrine.
  • Komachi-dori Street (Food, about 1 hr): Street food and Kamakura souvenirs.
  • Kotoku-in Great Buddha (Temple & shrine, about 0.5 hr): On some days you can go inside the Buddha.
  • Hase-dera Temple (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): The lookout gives a view over Yuigahama Beach.
  • Enoshima Island (Scenic view, about 2.5 hr): Caves, the Sea Candle lighthouse and shirasu bowls.

Hidden gems in Kamakura

  • Meigetsu-in Temple (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): The hydrangea temple. The garden behind the round window opens only in certain seasons.
  • Zeniarai Benzaiten Shrine (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): Wash your money in the cave spring and it's said to multiply.
  • Kakuon-ji Temple (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): Hidden deep in a valley, a glimpse of untouched Kamakura.
  • Hokoku-ji bamboo garden (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): Sip matcha while gazing at the bamboo grove.
  • Goryo Shrine & the Enoden (Temple & shrine, about 0.5 hr): A famous photo spot where the Enoden train passes the torii.
  • Gokuraku-ji Temple (Temple & shrine, about 0.5 hr): A thatched gate, and the retro Gokurakuji Station nearby.
  • Inamuragasaki (Scenic view, about 0.5 hr): On clear evenings Mt. Fuji and Enoshima line up at sunset.

Local food to try

Shirasu-don (whitebait bowl), Kamakura vegetables, Hato Sable cookies, Shojin ryori (temple food)

Where to stay

Most people day-trip, but staying near Kamakura Station or Enoshima lets you enjoy quiet mornings and evenings.
