Must-see spots in Kyoto

  • Kiyomizu-dera Temple (Temple & shrine, about 1.5 hr): Walk down Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka to Yasaka Pagoda.
  • Gion Shirakawa & Hanamikoji (Strolling, about 1 hr): Beautiful at dusk with lanterns on the stone paths. Please respect maiko and don't chase them for photos.
  • Fushimi Inari Taisha (Temple & shrine, about 2 hr): The further you climb through the torii gates, the fewer people. The view opens up at Yotsutsuji.
  • Nishiki Market (Food, about 1 hr): Snack your way through pickles, rolled omelet and yuba.
  • Nijo Castle (History, about 1.5 hr): Listen for the squeaky 'nightingale floors' of Ninomaru Palace.
  • Ginkaku-ji & the Philosopher's Path (Temple & shrine, about 2 hr): Follow the canal path all the way to Nanzen-ji.
  • Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): Sunny mornings make the gold shine brightest on the pond.
  • Arashiyama Bamboo Grove & Togetsukyo Bridge (Nature, about 2 hr): The bamboo grove is quiet early in the morning. Pair it with the Sagano Romantic Train.

Hidden gems in Kyoto

  • Rokudo Chinno-ji Temple (Temple & shrine, about 0.5 hr): A mysterious temple with a well said to lead to the underworld.
  • Tofuku-ji Temple (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): The autumn leaves from Tsutenkyo Bridge are among Kyoto's best. Quiet and lovely in spring green too.
  • Fushimi sake breweries (Food, about 1.5 hr): Taste sake at the Gekkeikan Okura Museum and ride a Jikkokubune boat.
  • Kyoto Railway Museum (Museum, about 2 hr): The SL Steam train ride on a real steam locomotive is a favorite.
  • Shimogamo Shrine & Tadasu no Mori (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): A cool path through old-growth forest. Birthplace of mitarashi dango.
  • Keibunsha bookshop in Ichijoji (Shopping, about 1 hr): A famous, beautifully curated bookshop. The area is a ramen hotspot.
  • Sanzen-in Temple, Ohara (Temple & shrine, about 2.5 hr): Adorable little stone Jizo statues in a moss garden. About 1 hour by bus.
  • Kifune Shrine (Temple & shrine, about 2 hr): Known for fortune slips that appear on water. Riverside dining platforms in summer.
  • Funaoka Onsen (Hot spring, about 1 hr): A public bath registered as a cultural property. Don't miss the carved panels in the changing room.
  • Gio-ji Temple (Temple & shrine, about 0.5 hr): A small nunnery with a beautiful carpet of moss.
  • Adashino Nenbutsu-ji Temple (Temple & shrine, about 0.5 hr): About 8,000 stone Buddhas and stupas. Walk the old Saga-Toriimoto street too.

Local food to try

Yudofu (simmered tofu), Obanzai home-style dishes, Nishin soba (herring soba), Matcha sweets

Where to stay

Stay around Kyoto Station, or around Shijo-Kawaramachi and Gion where you can walk to the sights.
