Must-see spots in Tokyo

  • Senso-ji Temple & Nakamise Street (Temple & shrine, about 1.5 hr): Come around 8 am for fewer people and better photos.
  • Tokyo Skytree (Scenic view, about 2 hr): Buy timed tickets for the Tembo Deck in advance to skip the line.
  • Ueno Park & Tokyo National Museum (Museum, about 2.5 hr): A full day of things to do, even when it rains.
  • Tsukiji Outer Market (Food, about 1.5 hr): Liveliest from morning to noon. Graze your way through breakfast.
  • teamLab Planets (Art, about 2 hr): Some rooms have you walk through water, so wear clothes you can roll up.
  • Tokyo Tower (Scenic view, about 1.5 hr): Frame it with Zojo-ji Temple for the classic shot.
  • Meiji Jingu Shrine (Temple & shrine, about 1.5 hr): A deep forest in the middle of the city. Lovely in the early morning.
  • Shibuya Crossing & SHIBUYA SKY (Scenic view, about 1.5 hr): Book SHIBUYA SKY early if you want the sunset slot.
  • Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden (Nature, about 1.5 hr): Seasonal flowers and wide lawns for a slow afternoon.
  • Mt. Takao (Nature, about 4 hr): Take the cable car and even beginners can reach the top easily.

Hidden gems in Tokyo

  • Kappabashi Kitchen Town (Shopping, about 1 hr): A street of kitchenware and plastic food sample shops. Fun even just to browse.
  • Kuramae craft workshops (Hands-on, about 1.5 hr): Small studios where you can make your own leather goods or notebooks.
  • Shibamata Taishakuten Temple (Temple & shrine, about 2 hr): The temple's carved wooden gallery is stunning. Try kusa-dango on the approach street.
  • Yanaka Ginza & Yanesen walk (Strolling, about 2 hr): Famous for the view from the Yuyake Dandan steps and alleys full of cats.
  • Kyu-Furukawa Gardens (Nature, about 1 hr): A Western mansion, a rose garden and a Japanese garden all in one.
  • Jimbocho used-book district (Shopping, about 1.5 hr): Browse the old bookshops, then have curry at a long-running local spot.
  • Kiyosumi Garden & Kiyosumi-Shirakawa cafes (Nature, about 1.5 hr): A stroll garden where you hop across stepping stones on the pond.
  • Meguro Parasitological Museum (Museum, about 1 hr): One of the world's few museums devoted to parasites. Free entry.
  • Shimokitazawa vintage & record shops (Strolling, about 2 hr): A subculture neighborhood packed with small theaters and live music venues.
  • Todoroki Valley (Nature, about 1 hr): The only ravine in Tokyo's 23 wards, a short walk from the station.
  • Omoide Yokocho & Golden Gai (Food, about 2 hr): Drop in for a drink at night in tiny bars packed side by side.

Local food to try

Monjayaki (Tsukishima), Edomae sushi, Tendon tempura bowl (Asakusa), Fukagawa-meshi clam rice (Kiyosumi-Shirakawa)

Where to stay

Asakusa or Ueno for old-town sightseeing; Shinjuku or Shibuya for shopping and nightlife.
