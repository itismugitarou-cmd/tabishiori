Must-see spots in Hakone

  • Hakone-Yumoto onsen town (Hot spring, about 1.5 hr): Snack on onsen manju along the shopping street by the station.
  • Hakone Open-Air Museum (Art, about 2 hr): Soak your feet in a foot bath while looking at sculpture.
  • Owakudani (Scenic view, about 1 hr): Each famous black egg is said to add seven years to your life.
  • Pola Museum of Art (Art, about 2 hr): The forest walking trail is lovely too.
  • Lake Ashi pirate ship (Scenic view, about 1 hr): On a clear day Mt. Fuji is right there.
  • Hakone Shrine (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): Expect a line to photograph the Torii of Peace on the lake.

Hidden gems in Hakone

  • Yosegi marquetry workshop in Hatajuku (Hands-on, about 1.5 hr): Make a coaster with traditional yosegi wood inlay.
  • Old Tokaido stone road & Amazake-chaya (History, about 2 hr): Walk an Edo-period stone road and rest at a thatched teahouse.
  • Gora Park (Nature, about 1 hr): A French-style garden around a fountain, with craft workshops.
  • Sengokuhara Pampas Grass Field (Nature, about 1 hr): A sea of gold in autumn, best around October.
  • Hakone Glass Forest Museum (Art, about 1.5 hr): A garden glittering with Venetian glass.
  • 500 Rakan statues at Chomei-ji (Temple & shrine, about 0.5 hr): Expressive Buddhist statues scattered through the forest.

Local food to try

Black eggs (kuro-tamago), Yuba rice bowl, Hakone soba, Tofu dishes

Where to stay

Yumoto is close to the station; Gora and Sengokuhara have more quiet inns.
