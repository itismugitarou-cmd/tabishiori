Must-see spots in Sendai

  • Jozenji-dori & Sendai Mediatheque (Strolling, about 1 hr): A zelkova avenue and a library-gallery designed by Toyo Ito.
  • Zuihoden (History, about 1 hr): Date Masamune's mausoleum, with brilliantly colored decoration.
  • Sendai Castle ruins (History, about 1 hr): The Date Masamune statue and a sweeping view of the city.
  • Matsushima (Scenic view, about 3 hr): Take a sightseeing cruise and visit Zuigan-ji and Godaido. Try the oysters.
  • Akiu Onsen (Hot spring, about 2 hr): Sendai's mountain retreat. Many inns offer day bathing.

Hidden gems in Sendai

  • Sendai Asaichi Market (Food, about 1 hr): A small market near the station. Freshly fried croquettes are a hit.
  • Rinno-ji Temple (Temple & shrine, about 1 hr): A temple tied to the Date family, with a beautiful pond garden.
  • Osaki Hachimangu Shrine (Temple & shrine, about 0.5 hr): A National Treasure hall in black lacquer and vivid colors.
  • Shiogama Shrine (Temple & shrine, about 1.5 hr): The top shrine of old Mutsu Province. Famous sushi shops line its gate town.
  • Sakunami Onsen (Hot spring, about 2 hr): Riverside open-air baths along the Hirose River.
  • Akiu Great Falls (Nature, about 1 hr): One of Japan's top 100 waterfalls. You can walk down near the basin.

Local food to try

Gyutan (grilled beef tongue), Zunda mochi, Sasa-kamaboko, Seri nabe hot pot

Where to stay

Sendai Station to Kokubuncho is convenient. Akiu or Sakunami Onsen make for a treat.
