Must-see spots in Nagasaki

  • Peace Park & Atomic Bomb Museum (Museum, about 2 hr): Pause quietly before the Peace Statue.
  • Dejima (History, about 1.5 hr): Restored Edo-period buildings of the old Dutch trading post.
  • Nagasaki Shinchi Chinatown (Food, about 1 hr): Snack on kakuni manju pork buns.
  • Oura Cathedral & Glover Garden (History, about 2 hr): Western mansions looking down on the harbor.
  • Mt. Inasa night view (Scenic view, about 1.5 hr): One of the world's new top three night views. Take the slope car up.
  • Gunkanjima (Battleship Island) cruise (Scenic view, about 3 hr): Landing may be canceled depending on sea conditions.

Hidden gems in Nagasaki

  • Meganebashi Bridge & heart stone (Strolling, about 0.5 hr): Look for the heart-shaped stone hidden in the river wall.
  • Kofuku-ji Temple (Temple & shrine, about 0.5 hr): Japan's oldest Chinese-style temple, with a vermilion gate.
  • Kameyama Shachu Memorial Museum (History, about 0.5 hr): Sakamoto Ryoma's base, up a hillside.
  • Dutch Slope & Higashiyamate (Strolling, about 1 hr): Meiji-era Western houses along a cobbled slope.
  • Shitsu village & Endo Shusaku Museum (History, about 3 hr): A hidden Christian village and sunsets over the sea.

Local food to try

Champon noodles, Sara udon, Turkish rice, Castella cake

Where to stay
