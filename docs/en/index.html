How Tabishiori works

Tabishiori is a free trip planner for Japan. Enter where you start, where you want to go and how many days you have, and it builds three day-by-day itineraries: one with the classic highlights, one with hidden gems, and one in between. It covers all 1,894 cities, towns and villages in Japan, with hand-picked spots for popular destinations like Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka. Each plan includes estimated travel time and budget, a weather forecast, a packing list and links to find a place to stay.
